PRESS RELEASE - FOR IMMEDIATE PUBLIC RELEASE
NEW YORK & LONDON - October 23, 2026

GLOBAL BANKING CORPORATION REPORTS STRONG FISCAL THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS
Net Income Up 14% Year-over-Year Driven by Core Retail Deposits and Asset Wealth Growth

NEW YORK -- Global Banking Corporation today announced financial operating results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

Key Performance Highlights:
- Total operating revenue reached $42.8 billion, representing a 9% increase over Q3 2025.
- Net income expanded to $8.9 billion, supported by sustained capital strength and disciplined expense control.
- Return on Tangible Common Equity (ROTCE) stood at 18.2%, reflecting strong performance across all business segments.
- Standard Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) capital ratio reached 15.1%, remaining well above Basel III regulatory standards.

Chief Executive Officer Statement:
"Our third-quarter performance reflects the resilience of our franchise and the trust of our institutional and retail clients worldwide. We maintained healthy loan growth while executing strategic investments in artificial intelligence, digital banking resilience, and autonomous data protection."

Operational Developments:
- Client deposits across all geographic sectors increased by $34 billion during the quarter.
- Commercial lending pipelines remained healthy across North American infrastructure initiatives.
- Digital active banking users grew to over 48 million active accounts globally.

About Global Banking Corporation:
Global Banking Corporation is a leading global financial services firm providing investment banking, consumer credit, wealth management, and treasury solutions to corporations, institutions, and individuals across more than 90 countries.
